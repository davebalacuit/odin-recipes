Shrimp Scampi Recipe

Description

This simple but flavorful Scampi is sure to be a big hit.

Ingredients

  • Medium shrimp
  • Olive oil
  • Melted butter
  • Minced garlic
  • Kosher salt
  • Ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat an oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Toss the shrimp in a bowl with the olive oil, melted butter, garlic, salt, and pepper; set aside for 10 minutes.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until the shrimp are pink and cooked through, about 15 minutes.
